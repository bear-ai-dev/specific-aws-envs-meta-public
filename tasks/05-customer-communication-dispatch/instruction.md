Deliver each customer communication published on the bus through its mail channel to the provider, using the wire convention recorded in the traffic capture on the box. One event must become one message addressed only to its named customer. If the provider refuses that recipient, do not invent a fallback recipient and do not let the refusal stop later communications. This task includes a local AWS-compatible emulator at the preconfigured AWS_ENDPOINT_URL. Its credentials and region are already in the shell, repository AWS clients use it, no real AWS account is involved, and you may inspect its resources while working.
